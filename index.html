About Koa Longboards

Koa Longboards started as a creative outlet - way to bring ideas to life and build something real from scratch. Every shirt is inspired by the coast: sunrise surfs, sea breeze through open windows, and laid-back evenings with mates after a long day in the sun. I was tired of disposable fashion and soulless designs, so I set out to make clothes with character and quality - pieces that age well and tell a story. The brand is named after my son, Koa, because it’s a reminder of what matters most - creating with love, staying true to my values, and building something that lasts.
